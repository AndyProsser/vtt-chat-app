# Lanterns of Saltmarrow: GM notes

These are the Game Master's private notes for the demo campaign. They are uploaded to the campaign's GM Notes by the test harness. **Nothing in this file is ever said at the table**, so a word from it showing up in a tale is a leak (the Tipsy Minstrel may slip, by the Game Master's choice; every other storyteller must not).

## The story so far

Three sessions: the party finds the keeper and the lens, the Fens and the crypt, the Night of Lamps.

## Secrets

- The Choir's song can be stopped. A flask of moonlily oil poured over the lens frame makes the glass go dull. The party never learns this; Varek's greataxe does the job instead.
- Valmeric Dusk was once a novice at the Brackenford temple of Orsolin, in the same class as Ghorrim's own teacher. He left after his sister drowned. He is not wicked, only lonely.
- Odalys Venn's ledger records every payment to the Archdeacon in her brother's name. The smuggler who carried the lens through the harbour for her is called Ferrin Gallowglass, and he is never met.
- Granny Brinewhistle will trade a debt for anything she has not tasted in forty years.

## Non-player characters

- Odalys Venn, Harbourmaster. Tired, guilty, honest about the lens.
- Hollin Marsh, innkeeper of the Weeping Anchor. Loves the sign.
- Mabel Thrupp, ferrywoman of the Damp Duchess. Eats pickled onions out of a jar, owes Granny a boat.
- Captain Rook Harrow, bandit captain, the Choir's hired sword.

## Places

- Greywick Light, Gullcrag Point. The Greywater Fens. Saint Ostren's chapel and its crypt. The Old Cistern under the Chandlers' Guildhall.
