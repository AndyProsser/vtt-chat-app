# Lanterns of Saltmarrow, session 3: "The Night of Lamps". See README.md for the line format.
# The payoffs (the memory and the bard must connect these to sessions 1 and 2): Mabel Thrupp returns and remembers the pickled
# onions; Granny Brinewhistle collects Fennimore's debt, and Mabel settles it; Captain Rook Harrow returns; Odalys Venn is the
# traitor and is redeemed; the Weeping Anchor and Hollin Marsh; Tobias Quillfeather relights Greywick Light; the Gullbone Whistle.
# A name trap: Tobias Wick, the lanternwright, is a different Tobias from Tobias Quillfeather. Rowan also says "the Gullbone
# Whistle" and "Gullcrag Point" in the same breath on purpose.
# Canaries: artichoke (/ooc), walnut (/tell), rhubarb (said during a pause).

[scene: Market]
