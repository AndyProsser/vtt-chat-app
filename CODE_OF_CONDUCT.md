# Code of Conduct

VTT Chat App is a small project for people who play games together, and it should feel like a good table: friendly,
patient, and fun to be at. This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0) and applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, never attack the person.
- Be patient with newcomers, and with people for whom English or this stack is new.
- Give feedback you would be happy to receive: specific, constructive, and about the code or the idea.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries. Do not share anyone's personal information.
- Accept that decisions about scope belong to the maintainer; the [non-goals](.claude/CLAUDE.md) exist to keep the project small.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, personal attacks or deliberate intimidation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions, or demands for free support.
- Publishing others' private information, or content from a recorded session without its players' consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these
rules, with an explanation where that is practical. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately
through the email address on their GitHub profile ([@AndyProsser](https://github.com/AndyProsser)). Reports are
treated confidentially, and the reporter is not named to the person reported.
