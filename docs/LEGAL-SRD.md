## What we use it for

The item catalog behind the Party inventory (`shared/src/srd/data.generated.ts`): the names, types,
rarities, prices and one-sentence descriptions of the SRD's **magic items** and **mundane equipment**.
It is generated by `scripts/srd/build.ts` (`npm run srd:build`) from a pinned commit of a public
Markdown copy of the SRD, and the generated file carries the attribution in its header. The app shows
the attribution line in the Party inventory window.

## What we do not use

The SRD 5.1 does **not** contain the Dungeon Master's Guide treasure tables, and the DMG is not open
content, so we have not copied its tables or its magic-item lists beyond what the SRD itself contains.
Random treasure uses our own generator — see [architecture/LOOT.md](architecture/LOOT.md).
